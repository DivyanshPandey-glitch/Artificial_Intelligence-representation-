Artificial Intelligence Classification, Working Mechanism and Future Potential

From the perspective of artificial intelligence, BRAHMA, in its current form, is best classified as a Narrow AI (Weak AI) system, rather than Artificial General Intelligence (AGI).

Narrow AI refers to artificial intelligence designed to perform specific tasks within defined capabilities, whereas AGI would possess a much broader ability to understand, learn, and solve unfamiliar problems across different domains at a human-like general level.

BRAHMA uses an integration-based approach in which different AI technologies and software components cooperate to produce intelligent behaviour.

How Does BRAHMA Work?

1. User Input: The user provides a command through voice or text.
2. Speech Recognition: If the command is spoken, a speech-recognition system converts the audio into text.
3. Natural Language Processing: An AI model interprets the user's intention and understands the requested operation.
4. Task Identification: The system identifies the appropriate function, Python module, or API required.
5. Execution: The relevant component performs the authorized task.
6. Response Generation: The result is processed and communicated back to the user.
7. Voice Output: Text-to-speech technologies, such as ElevenLabs, can generate natural-sounding spoken responses.

Depending on its implementation, BRAHMA can also use conditional logic, automation scripts, machine-learning models, and connected services to improve its functionality.

This integrated approach represents an important step towards a more connected and intelligent digital environment. Rather than requiring users to operate dozens of applications independently, BRAHMA aims to bring their essential capabilities together in one assistant.

It represents a technological revolution in the way humans interact with computers and mobile devices, making everyday digital activities more accessible, productive, convenient, and effortless.
